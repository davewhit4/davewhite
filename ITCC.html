Sour Dough Bread

Sour Dough Bread is cheap to make, make as much or as little as you like. Take the mess and hard work out of it by using a breadmaker for the mixing and kneading.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Water (tepid)
  • Milk
  • Vegetable Oil

Cooking instructions

  1. Add Flour and half the weight of tepid water
  2. Mix together into dough
  3. Put into plastic container
  4. Cover with lid but allow air to get in
  5. Leave over night (it turns into sour dough)
  6. Add twice the amount of flour and water than the first batch
  7. Add tablespoon of milk powder (or milk)
  8. Add two tablespoons of cooking oil
  9. Knead into dough
  10. Allow to rise to twice its current size, then bake for half an hour
